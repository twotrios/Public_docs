TwoTrios – Delete your account

App: TwoTrios · Developer: Raul Carreira Rufato

You can delete your TwoTrios account and the data linked to it at any time, either inside the app or by email.

Option 1: delete it in the app

  1. Open TwoTrios.
  2. On the home screen, tap your email address under your star count.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete.

The account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: ask by email

Send an email to twotriosgame@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my TwoTrios account”, from the email address of the account you want deleted. The account is deleted within 30 days, and you receive a confirmation by email.

If you play as a guest (you never added an email address in the app), your account has no email or name attached to it. To have it deleted, first add your email in the app under Save or load your stars, then use either option above.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its email address.
  • Your stars, including any stars you bought. They cannot be restored.
  • Your game reward history linked to the account.

What is kept

  • Records of purchases and of rewarded ads are kept without any link to you (the account identifier is removed), to prevent fraud and to meet legal accounting obligations.
  • Messages you sent through HELP in the app are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted automatically. Ask by email if you want them removed sooner.
  • Payment details are held by Google Play, not by TwoTrios. Data used for advertising is held by Google AdMob; you can change your ad choices with PRIVACY on the app's home screen.
